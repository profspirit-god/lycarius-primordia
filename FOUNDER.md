## 1. What this game is

- **One sentence:** a sandbox where you start with nothing and become whatever you choose (mage, craftsman, merchant, leader) in a world that reacts to what players do.
- **Player fantasy:** "Nobody tells me what to be."
- **Genre references:** sandbox and life-sim MMOs (Ultima Online, Albion Online, EVE, Eco, Wurm), not a fixed-loop theme-park. A world, and players make their own loop.
- **Platforms:** browser first, packaged later as a desktop build (`SERVER_PATH.md` S10). The game is not browser-only.
- **Owner goal:** enjoy the process, build the dream game, earn, learn, finish what is started.
- **Relation to `Claude.md`:** the long-term list there (factions, guild wars, branching story) stays the long-term vision. Section 8 only says what is **not built now**.

## 2. Design laws

Every feature handed to a Claude session respects these.

1. **Primitives, not paths.** Build a small set of shared primitives. Every career (cook, swordsman, merchant, leader) is the same primitives wired differently, so careers are mostly **data**, not new systems.
2. **Depth over breadth.** A new career or class must be as deep as combat already is (charge curves, mana previews, status effects, projectile clash, AI modes, a spec tree). The bar is the existing Mage kit. A shallow career is a vending machine.
3. **Multiplayer is last.** Deferred, not cancelled. Code stays server-ready (`Claude.md` 5b) so it can come later. Prove the solo loop first. The signal is a friend saying "I wish I could sell you this thing I found."
4. **Pressure, not erasure.** Hunger, death and failure make you worse or set you back. They never delete identity or progress.
5. **Every system is a reason to eat, or a reason to cook.** Content earns its place by deepening the loop, not by adding surface area.
6. **Freedom, never requirement.** Optional systems (building, stations) give efficiency and quality, never access.

## 3. Working rhythm

Suggested habits recorded from the first draft. Session mechanics (changes-only zip, tests, one step per version) are in `Claude.md` section 2 and win on any difference.

- **Zero days.** Move the progress bar a little every day. A dated `CHANGELOG.md` entry counts. Low-energy days can be a conversation about a system or a proposal in `docs/proposals/`.
- **Slot sizes.** 1 slot (about 15 min of agent work): read, plan, list, changelog. 3 slots: a small feature and its tests. 20 slots (rare): a full loop end to end, or a spike.
- **Version A / Version B.** A is the live repo, one in-progress milestone at a time, never two at once. B is a fork for spikes and experiments and never touches main.
- **Plan before code** for any new system: a plan-only session, then a build session, then a tuning session.

## 5. Death law

**Death is a setback, not a reset.** It costs time and goods, never identity.

| Stage | Cost of death | Status |
| ----- | ------------- | ------ |
| Now | Full-health respawn at the start (current code) | Placeholder |
| Next | Respawn **delay** in seconds (a tuning constant) | Build soon, small |
| When carried items exist | Lose what you **carry**, keep bank / home | Target model |
| Never | XP loss | Rejected: punishes casual players, makes no stories |
| Opt-in only | Permadeath dungeon modifier | Later, never the base rule |

**Why not from zero:** every career in the fantasy (craftsman, businessman, leader) needs persistence. Goods-loss is the sandbox standard and gives the merchant career its depth: do you risk carrying your goods to market?

## 6. Hunger law and spec

**Hunger is pressure, not erasure.** It escalates through efficiency, movement, regeneration and recoverable conditions. **Hunger may damage, but it can never kill** (owner resolution R12): hunger-caused damage is non-lethal (never below 1 HP), bounded and only ever comes from statuses, never from a tier. It is the keystone of the cooking career. Built as track H, with its statuses in track HS (`ROADMAP.md`, `docs/proposals/Hunger-statuses.md`).

### 6a. Stat, timing and tiers

- Internally a 0 to 1000 stat of **hunger points** (R16), shown on the HUD as a **percentage** (`floor(value / max * 100)`). It starts at 850 (85%, Full: a new character is not born Stuffed, R11) and drains **1 point per 9 s of active play** (100% to 0% in 9000 s, 2.5 hours). Drain uses the sim clock (`dt`), never a wall clock, and does not run while the game is closed, paused or the player is downed.
- A tier applies the moment the value reaches its minimum. Tiers change effects immediately; the status icons come from the tier and the active statuses.
- **Regeneration is separate per stat** (R18): HP, mana and stamina each have their own multiplier. Mana stays at 1.0 in every tier until the owner sets values. The numbers below are **provisional** (H1 values, tuned later).

| Tier | Range | HP regen | Stamina regen | Mana regen | Move speed |
| ---- | ----- | -------- | ------------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| Stuffed | 90% to 100% | +5% | 1.0 | 1.0 | -10% |
| Full | 60% to 89% | +5% | 1.0 | 1.0 | none |
| Not hungry | 25% to 59% | baseline | baseline | baseline | none |
| Hungry | 8% to 24% | -25% | -25% | 1.0 | -5% |
| Starving | 0% to 7% | -50% | -50% | 1.0 | -15% |

Temporary max-HP effects per tier (a bonus when Stuffed, a reduction when Starving) are wanted but wait for system row X2 and magnitudes. The code (`src/sim/hunger/`, H1b, V1.22.0.00) follows this table in points: Stuffed 900, Full 600, Not hungry 250, Hungry 80, Starving 0.

### 6b. Guardrails

- **Never kills:** hunger damage never takes HP below 1, never downs the player, never stacks, and ends when its cause ends or the player is downed (`docs/proposals/Hunger-statuses.md` 2c).
- **Move speed:** each tier slows by at most 15%, and **all hunger sources together never slow below 75% of normal** (R14). The floor applies to the product, so statuses cannot push past it.
- **Regeneration is never switched off** and the lowest tier can always eat: food is never a soft-lock. Eating takes about 1 s.
- The real teeth are speed, stamina and recovery, **not** the HP regen number: base regen (0.1 HP/s) is so weak that -50% is invisible. Whether to buff base regen so hunger bites is still a later balance decision.
- Hunger and its multipliers are plain sim data (`Claude.md` 5b R8); modifiers carry an owner key so one source never removes another's; saves go through the storage adapter.
- On respawn the stat is raised to at least **25%** (R20); it is never lowered by death.

### 6c. Eating and food (v1)

- **Food has a point value.** `eat(points)` is allowed whenever the stat is below 100% and no Overeating is active. If `stat + points` would exceed the maximum, the stat becomes 100%, the excess is wasted, and **Overeating** starts (R13). While the stat is at 100% or Overeating is active, eating is refused and the food is kept.
- Eating in the Stuffed tier is allowed: it is the risk. Each successful eat that ends in Stuffed draws once from the player's Stuffed bag (`Hunger-statuses.md` 2a).
- **Raw Meat:** Graugr drop at about 25% (through `LootSystem`, V1.21.0.00), +150 points (15%). **Cooked Meat:** raw + campfire + 2 s, +450 points (45%) and **+5% HP regen for 60 s** (the campfire baseline; stations in `BUILD.md` change the amount and add a chance of a random buff). Point values are provisional (R16) and tuned in H3.
- The gap between raw and cooked is the incentive to cook. One recipe teaches the loop: kill, drop, cook, eat, fight better.
- **Deferred:** an injury system (persistent wounds separate from HP), parked in `docs/proposals/Injuries.md` (to be written when it is picked up).

### 6d. Statuses

The hunger statuses (Overeating, Bloated, Famished, Perishing and the rest) are specified, with their bags, timers and the systems they need, in `docs/proposals/Hunger-statuses.md`. Each is gated by the system rows it needs; the system comes first (R19).

## 7. Food purpose ranking

All six purposes are real but not equal.

| # | Purpose | Needs first | Verdict |
| - | ------- | ----------- | ------- |
| 1 | **Hunger bar** | `Vitals`, `StatusSystem` (built) | **Keystone: build now** |
| 2 | Starving NPC / drought donation | quest system | Later (the quest primitive's first use) |
| 3 | Faction anniversary cooking event | factions, events, multiplayer | Deferred |
| 4 | Master cook competition | dialogue, quest chains | Later (a solo "beat the score" is the cheap entry) |
| 5 | Merchant / cook-seller | trade, economy, multiplayer | Deferred |
| 6 | War: bring your own food | war zones, multiplayer | Deferred: the payoff of #1 |

**Insight:** #1 makes food matter. #2 to #6 are reasons to care, and each needs a system that does not exist yet. Build the keystone; the rest become demand generators. #6 is the depth tree: hunger in peacetime is a nudge, hunger in war turns food into logistics.

## 8. Not building now (anti-drift)

Written down so a session cannot wander. "Now" means before the milestone in section 9 is proven.

- Multiplayer, servers and accounts as the **active** track (the server path continues as a background track, one step at a time; see section 12 R1).
- **Shallow** extra classes. Deep classes are wanted, at lower priority than the milestone (R5).
- Art production beyond what an effect needs (the art track stays planned and low priority, R3).
- Campaign story, factions, guilds, economy simulation, ownership and territory.
- In-game meetings or real-world integrations.
- The account Shop buy flow (wallet, L4) and Genesis Path rewards (R4). John the Merchant's stat tickets (V1.21.0.00) are the only in-world sale and stay as they are.
- The injury system. Permadeath as a base rule.
- Any hunger **status** whose system row (`ROADMAP.md` track HS) is not built: the system first, then the status.
- Walls, rooms, decay and land claims (`BUILD.md` Stages 2 and 3).
